## Interests
Phonology, poetry, Basque, historical linguistics.

### Narrower interests
Rhyme (and other poetic agreements) as harmony, subregular models of phonological maps, evolution of Basque, cognitive requirements of Basque improvised poetry duels (*bertsolaritza*).

## Experience & Fieldwork
- **[Research Centre for Basque Language and Texts](https://iker.cnrs.fr/?lang=en) (Fall 2026)**: Visiting researcher at IKER (CNRS UMR5478) in Bayonne, France; experimentation on rhyme phonology in Basque oral poetry (*bertsolaritza*).
- **[Caucasian Languages in Georgia](https://sites.google.com/view/caulage2025summerprogram/home) (Summer 2025)**: Student in the CauLaGe summer program in Zemo Alvani, Georgia; researching and documenting Tsova-Tush (Nakh-Daghestani); analysis of poetic rhyme.
- **[Pragmatics & Discourse at IU](https://pragmatics.indiana.edu) (2021–2023)**: Research assistant in Bloomington, IN; audio processing, transcription, data entry for Spanish/English pragmatics research; copyediting; website maintenance.
- **[The Language Conservancy](https://languageconservancy.org) (Summer 2021)**: Linguistics intern in Bloomington, IN; revitalization work for Cowlitz Coast Salish (ƛ̕púlmixq); lemma revision in Cowlitz-English bilingual dictionary.
